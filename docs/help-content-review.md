Reviewed on 25 October 2026 against the current conversion tool code, including the customer list export sample and 3,000-record parser test. The category/article layout is retained. | Subject | Confirmed behaviour and documentation adjustment | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upload requirements | No single upload field is mandatory. Check files requires a selection; invoice rows require at least one supported invoice CSV. | Customer CSV and addresses | **Customer List Upload** reads JobID, Customer, Tel, Mobile, Email and Address, including address continuations in unnamed trailing columns. Uploading does not create invoice rows or Sage contacts, or fill Sage addresses. | `src/customerList.ts`, `src/customerList.test.ts`; `showCustomerListResult`, `renderCustomerListPreview` in `functions/[[path]].ts` |
